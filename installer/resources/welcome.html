HEXA FM

A six-operator FM synthesizer with a sound library built for finding, sorting and keeping sounds. More than 1,300 sounds are included.

On the next pages you'll see the terms and a short explanation of the GNU GPL v3 licence. Then click Customize to choose what to install:

  • Hexa FM app: the standalone synthesizer
  • Audio Unit: for Logic Pro, GarageBand and MainStage
  • VST3: for Ableton Live, Cubase, Studio One and most other music software
  • CLAP: for Bitwig Studio, Reaper and other CLAP hosts

The old VST2 format isn't available because Steinberg no longer licenses it. The manual (Hexa FM Manual.pdf, also on the disk image) explains installation, macOS security prompts and how to use the synth.
