Hexa FM is installed

Open Hexa FM from Applications or Launchpad. If macOS asks whether to open it, choose Open. If it blocks it, go to System Settings › Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway.

In your music software, restart it or rescan plugins to find Hexa FM among your instruments. If the Audio Unit doesn't appear in Logic, quit Logic, open it again and use Settings › Plug-in Manager › Reset & Rescan.

Chapter 3 of the manual covers every macOS security message step by step.
